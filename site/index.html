Analog Planets

Video publishing tool by Felipe Meneguzzi

Analog Planets is a video production tool for short-form analog horror videos. When a video is finished, it sends the file to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft. The creator then reviews, edits, adds a caption and publishes it inside the TikTok app.

How it works

  1. The creator connects their TikTok account through TikTok Login.
  2. The tool shows the name of the connected account.
  3. The creator runs the upload command for a finished video.
  4. The video arrives in the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  5. The creator opens the notification in the TikTok app and decides whether to publish.

Nothing is published automatically. Publishing always happens in the TikTok app, by the creator.
