Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains how Analog Planets, operated by Felipe Meneguzzi, handles data from connected TikTok accounts.

Data we receive from TikTok

  • An access token and a refresh token, used to send videos on your behalf.
  • Your TikTok open ID, a technical identifier for your account in this app.
  • Your display name, shown once to confirm which account was connected. It is not stored.

Analog Planets does not read your videos, followers, messages or statistics on TikTok.

How the data is used

The data is used only to connect the account and send the videos you choose to your TikTok inbox. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Where the data is stored

Tokens and the open ID are stored in a protected local file on the operator's computer. Analog Planets has no database and no server of its own.

Website

This website is static. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep technical access logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy.

Videos

Video files go directly from the operator's computer to TikTok. After that, they are subject to the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Retention and your rights

Tokens are kept until access is revoked or the local file is deleted. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings. Under Brazil's data protection law (LGPD), you can also ask for access to your data or for its deletion by writing to the address below.
